Personalized Care for Every Child

At Mom’s Star, we understand that every child with Autism and Developmental Delay has a unique story. Our holistic approach goes beyond traditional therapy sessions. We create personalized developmental roadmaps tailored to each child’s individual needs, abilities, and progress. Our commitment to continuous monitoring and adapting strategies ensures meaningful, long-term development. Join us in fostering a nurturing and supportive environment where every child can shine.

TAILORED THERAPY SOLUTIONS

Empowering children with autism

Speech therapy >

Speech Therapy helps children develop clear speech and effective communication skills. It supports language understanding, sentence formation, and correct pronunciation. Therapy improves social communication, listening, and interaction skills. Sessions are engaging and play-based to keep children motivated.

Occupational therapy >

Occupational Therapy helps children become independent in daily activities like playing, learning, and self-care. It improves motor skills, sensory processing, and confidence through engaging, play-based methods tailored to each child.

ABA therapy >

ABA Therapy is a research-based approach that improves communication, social, and daily living skills using positive reinforcement. It helps children gain independence and build meaningful behaviors step by step.
